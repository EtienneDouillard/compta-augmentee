# src/lib rules
- "Ne plus demander" rules live in `skip_rules` (per dossier, label words or supplier). `applySkipRules()` (sync.server.ts) runs after each sync and on rule creation: only open `pending` lines with no `skip_rule_id` are set to `dismissed`, so lines already sent, or reactivated by the cabinet, are left alone. Deleting a rule puts its still-dismissed lines back to `pending`.
- "Saisie augmentée" (`src/lib/saisie*.ts`, `src/components/SaisieAugmentee.tsx`): Claude reads an invoice uploaded by the client, `eclater()` (`src/lib/eclatement.ts`, taken from the hackathon app « Comptabilité augmentée ») proposes one account per nature with the 486 split, the firm validates. Claude picks each account in the ANC chart of accounts at 1 January 2026 (`src/data/pcg-2026.ts`, generated from the ANC PDF; `src/lib/pcg.ts`), and a validated account must belong to that chart or subdivide one of its purchase accounts. On a hackathon demo dossier only, a validated PDF invoice can be sent to Pennylane as a supplier invoice with its PDF through the MCP `run_operations` tool (`src/lib/pennylane-import*.ts`: PDF upload, invoice created from the PDF, then its lines replaced by the validated ones). The accounts must already be open in the Pennylane company for the line's VAT rate; Pennylane assigns the supplier itself. `mcpCallAllowed` checks the company in `path_params` for `run_operations`. Accounts validated for a tiers go to `regles_fournisseur` and win over the keyword rules next time. Each reading is logged in `ai_analysis_runs` (kind `saisie`) so the monthly Claude cap covers it.
- Client deposit check (`controlerDepot`, `etatDepots` in `saisie.functions.ts`; `src/lib/controle-depot.ts`; hook `src/lib/use-depot.ts`): right after a client deposit, Claude reads the purchase invoice once (same reading and `saisies` row as the firm's saisie augmentée, so it is never paid twice) and the app compares supplier and TTC with the payment. A payment only partly covered stays `a_faire` with "reste X € à justifier". The client's JWT proves access through RLS on `uploads`; the reading runs with the service role because `saisies` and `ai_analysis_runs` are firm tables. A failed or skipped reading never blocks the deposit.
- Envoi groupé (`listerEnvoiGroupe`, `envoyerGroupe`; `src/lib/envoi-groupe.ts`; `src/components/EnvoiGroupe.tsx`): the firm validates as proposed and sends every ready invoice of a demo dossier with one confirmation. `etatEnvoi()` keeps out anything that needs a human look (écart, account outside the ANC 2026 chart, non-PDF, already sent). Same demo-only rule as the single sending (Julien, 29/10/2026).
- Client screens: multi-supplier actions on the home page (`src/components/MultiActions.tsx`), suggested "ne plus demander" rules (`REGLES_CONSEILLEES` in `skip-rules.ts`, added by the firm in one click, never automatically). New lines still arrive `pending` and stay invisible to the client until the firm sends them.
- Écriture sans facture (`sans-facture.ts`, `sans-facture.functions.ts`, `src/components/EcritureSansFacture.tsx`): when the client answers « pas de facture », the firm picks the entry against the bank payment (charge without deductible VAT, 4551, 471, 4091 or another account of the ANC 2026 chart, never 512), saved in `transactions.ecriture_*` (SQL in `docs/migrations/2026-10-29-ecriture-sans-facture.sql`, client blocked by `guard_tx_update`). On a demo dossier only, it is booked on the Pennylane bank transaction with `postCreateLedgerEventsCompanyAccountantsTransaction`: the lines replace the counterpart and the amount is signed like the transaction (tested on 29/10/2026).
- Ventilation math and rules live in `src/lib/ventilation.ts`, tested in `src/test/ventilation.test.ts`, so UI and future Pennylane writes share one source of truth.
- Splitting an invoice by nature (one account per invoice line, multi-month services cut between expense and 486) lives in `src/lib/eclatement.ts`, tested in `src/lib/eclatement.test.ts`; invoice lines are read from the uploaded document by AI in `src/lib/piece.functions.ts`, because Pennylane does not expose invoice line details or PDFs.
- Claude's reading returns `type_document` (facture, avoir, proforma, devis, bon_de_commande, autre), kept in `saisies.lecture` and read back as `lecture->>typeDocument` (older readings count as facture). A proforma, a quote or an order form never counts towards the payment, keeps the request open and is never sent to Pennylane (`TYPE_NON_VALABLE`, `src/lib/type-document.ts`).
- Supplier request e-mail (`src/lib/demande-fournisseur.ts`) and "photo hors facture" (comment `PHOTO_SANS_FACTURE_COMMENT`, no Claude reading, no VAT) are client-side only: no e-mail is sent by the app, no column added.
- Supplier logos (`src/lib/logos.ts`): the favicon of the supplier's own site (invoice source URL, else a short list of well-known suppliers), loaded by the browser, initials as fallback; nothing stored, no logo service.
- Supplier sites are guessed once per dossier/supplier at sync time (`prefillSupplierSites`, table `supplier_sites`: bank label first, else Claude), so the setup screen opens pre-filled; the setup only searches when one is missing.
